# CamConnect Release Notes

</center>

## What's New in Your Camera App

Welcome to the latest version of CamConnect! We've packed this release with exciting features to make your camera experience smoother and more powerful.

## Available Features

### Smart Interface Layouts
Your camera app now adapts to how you want to use it:

- **Stream View** - Perfect for watching your camera feed with maximum screen space
- **Control View** - Balanced layout for easy access to camera controls while viewing
- **Settings View** - Full configuration panel for detailed camera adjustments

The app automatically switches between these views based on what you're doing!

### Recording Features
Capture everything happening on your screen:

- **Screen Recording** - Record your entire phone screen including the camera feed
- **One-tap Recording** - Simple start/stop with visual recording indicator
- **Auto-save to Gallery** - All recordings are automatically saved to your phone's gallery
- **High Quality** - Records in HD quality with smooth 30fps playback

### Camera Controls

#### Quick Camera Adjustments

- **Flip Camera** - Instantly flip your camera view horizontally or vertically (works immediately!)
- **Mirror View** - Mirror the camera image for selfie-style viewing (instant response!)

#### Advanced Camera Modes
When you switch to manual camera mode, the app will reload the stream with your new settings:

**Day Mode (Normal Lighting - IR-CUT-FILTER ON):**

- *Stable Mode* - Electronic Image Stabilization (EIS) ON for smooth, steady video
- *Dynamic Range* - Wide Dynamic Range (WDR) ON for maximum detail in bright and dark areas  
- *4K Vision* - EIS OFF, WDR OFF for ultra-high definition recording with maximum quality

**IR Mode (Night Vision - IR-CUT-FILTER OFF, IR ON):**

- *Stable Mode* - EIS ON, WDR OFF for steady night vision video
- *Dynamic Range* - EIS OFF, WDR ON for enhanced low-light visibility
- *4K Vision* - EIS OFF, WDR OFF for maximum quality night recording

**Low-Light Mode:**
- Optimized settings for challenging lighting conditions

### AI-Powered Detection
Smart features to help you see more:

- **Object Detection** - Automatically spots and highlights objects in your camera view (OFF by default)
- **Far Object Detection** - Detects objects at a distance (only available when Object Detection is ON)
- **Smart Overlays** - When AI is active, detection boxes scale perfectly with your zoom level

*Note: When you change AI settings, the camera stream will reload to apply the new detection features.*

### Night Vision (IR Control)
Perfect visibility even in complete darkness:

- **Smart Cycling** - Tap the IR button to cycle through brightness levels:
  - First tap → LOW intensity
  - Second tap → MEDIUM intensity  
  - Third tap → HIGH intensity
  - Fourth tap → MAX intensity
  - Fifth tap → ULTRA intensity
  - Sixth tap → OFF (cycles back to start)
- **Auto-adjustment** - Only activates in low-light or night conditions
- **Visual Feedback** - Button color changes to show current intensity level

### Status Indicators
Stay informed about your device and connection:

- **Battery Level** - Shows your phone's battery percentage with color-coded warnings
- **WiFi Signal** - Real-time WiFi connection strength with signal bars
- **Network Status** - See if you're connected and online
- **AI Status** - Shows when smart detection features are active

### Zoom & Navigation
Intuitive camera control:

- **Pinch to Zoom** - Use natural pinch gestures to zoom in/out on the camera feed (just like in your photo gallery!)
- **Smart Scaling** - When AI detection is active, all detection boxes zoom with the video
- **Double-tap Reset** - Double-tap to quickly return to normal zoom level
- **Smooth Panning** - Drag to move around when zoomed in

### Settings & Configuration
Easy access to all camera options:

- **Multiple Layout Views** - Switch between different control layouts based on your needs
- **Camera Settings** - Adjust resolution, quality, and recording options
- **AI Configuration** - Turn detection features on/off as needed
- **Theme Support** - Works great in both light and dark mode

## Tips for Best Experience

1. **For Recording**: Make sure you have enough storage space on your device
2. **For Night Vision**: IR works best in completely dark environments  
3. **For AI Detection**: Enable Object Detection first to unlock additional AI features
4. **For Zoom**: Use pinch gestures naturally - the app responds just like your photo gallery
5. **For Battery**: Keep an eye on the battery indicator during long recording sessions

## Privacy & Security
Your privacy is important to us. All camera feeds and recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
